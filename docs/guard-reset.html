Why this page exists

The Accountability Lock is a password that hands the controls of a device to somebody else. It is not stored on our servers and it is not something we can read, so there is nothing here to look up and nothing to email back.

What there is, if it was set up, is one address that reset links are sent to. Opening the link on this page is the whole of the proof: it says that whoever holds that inbox agrees to the lock coming off. Nobody sitting at the locked device can do that for themselves, which is the point of having handed the password over in the first place.

Taking the lock off changes nothing else. Schedules, history and any block already running all stay exactly as they were.
